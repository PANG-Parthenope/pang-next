# People

Version: 1.2

Status: Approved

---

# Purpose

The People page presents the members of the PArthenope Navigation Group (PANG).

The page introduces the research team through a clear, consistent and responsive layout while highlighting the different roles within the group.

---

# Layout

This page follows Decision D010.

Every page in PANG Next follows the same structure:

1. Page Title
2. Short Introduction
3. Main Content
4. Related Content (when applicable)
5. Footer

The page uses a full-width layout without sidebars.

---

# Page Header

## Title

People

## Introduction

The PArthenope Navigation Group (PANG) brings together faculty members, researchers, PhD students and collaborators from universities and research institutions working together in positioning, navigation, geomatics and naval architecture.

# Member Card

Each member is represented by a standard card.

Contents:

- Photo
- Full Name
- Academic Position
- Affiliation
- Category Badge
- View Profile button

Cards have identical dimensions and layout.

Cards intentionally exclude:

- Biography
- Publications
- Contact information
- Research interests

---

# Personal Profile
