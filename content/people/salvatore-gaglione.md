# Biography

Salvatore Gaglione is Full Professor at the University of Naples Parthenope and a member of the PArthenope Navigation Group (PANG).

His research activity focuses on positioning, navigation, geomatics and resilient Positioning, Navigation and Timing (PNT), with particular emphasis on Global Navigation Satellite Systems (GNSS), integrated navigation, maritime navigation and multi-sensor positioning systems.

He is Senior Vice President and President-elect of the International Association of Institutes of Navigation (IAIN)

He has participated in numerous national and international research projects and authored scientific publications in the fields of satellite navigation, positioning technologies and navigation systems.
